# DecodeLabs Internship - Project 4

## Project:
Data Visualization & Dashboard

## Tools Used:
- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel

## Dashboard Components:
- KPI Cards
  - Average Unit Price
  - Median Unit Price
  - Maximum Price
  - Minimum Price
  - Total Orders

- Total Sales by Product (Bar Chart)
- Orders Distribution by Payment Method (Pie Chart)
- Total Orders by Year (Line Chart)
- Product Filter (Slicer)

## Objectives:
- Analyze sales performance.
- Monitor order trends over time.
- Compare product sales.
- Explore payment method distribution.

## Key Insights:
- Chair and Printer achieved the highest sales.
- Phone recorded the lowest sales.
- Payment methods are relatively balanced.
- Order volume decreased from 2023 to 2025.
